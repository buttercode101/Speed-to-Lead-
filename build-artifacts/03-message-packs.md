# Niche Message Packs

## Shared rules
- Keep messages concise and human.
- Confirm service + area.
- Offer a clear next step.
- Include urgent path.

---

## Plumbing
### Instant acknowledgement
Hi {{FirstName}}, thanks for contacting {{BusinessName}}. We received your plumbing request in {{Area}}. A technician will call shortly. If this is urgent (burst pipe/no water), reply **URGENT**.

### First contact script
Hi {{FirstName}}, this is {{AgentName}} from {{BusinessName}}. I can assist with {{ServiceNeeded}} in {{Area}}. Can I confirm your address and best time for dispatch?

## Real Estate

Hi {{FirstName}}, thanks for your enquiry about {{PropertyOrService}} in {{Area}}. We’ve logged your request and an agent will contact you shortly. Reply **VIEWING** if you’d like the earliest slot.

Hi {{FirstName}}, {{AgentName}} here from {{AgencyName}}. Thanks for your interest in {{PropertyOrService}}. Are you buying/renting, and what timeline are you targeting?
