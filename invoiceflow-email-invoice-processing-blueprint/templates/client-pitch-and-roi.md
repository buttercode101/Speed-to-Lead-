# InvoiceFlow Client Pitch & ROI Pack

## One-Minute Pitch
“I help accountants and businesses stop wasting hours every week manually typing invoices. This automation reads invoice emails, extracts the data, puts it into your spreadsheet or accounting workflow, archives the file, and only sends you the tricky ones. Most clients save 25-40 hours per month.”

## Discovery Questions
1. How many supplier invoices, receipts, and statements do you receive per month?
2. Who currently opens the emails and captures the invoice details?
3. How long does one invoice usually take from email to captured record?
4. Where do exceptions happen most often: missing VAT, unreadable PDFs, duplicates, or coding/category decisions?
5. Do you need spreadsheet output only for V1, or should Xero/Sage posting be a later phase?

## ROI Example
```text
150 invoices/month x 10 minutes saved = 1,500 minutes saved
1,500 minutes / 60 = 25 hours saved
25 hours x R300-R400/hour = R7,500-R10,000 monthly value
```
