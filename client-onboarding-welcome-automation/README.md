# Client Onboarding & Welcome Automation Blueprint (SA Service Business V1)

A complete Make.com + Google Workspace automation blueprint for South African service-based businesses that want to turn signed clients into a smooth, professional onboarding experience.

## Core Value Proposition
Create an instant professional first impression. The moment a client submits an onboarding or agreement form, the system logs the client, creates their shared folder, copies welcome assets, sends a personalized welcome email, starts checklist tasks, issues the first invoice/payment link, and notifies the owner.

## Best Target Segments in South Africa
- Business consultants and coaches
- Marketing and digital agencies
- Accountants and bookkeepers
- HR consultants and training providers
- IT service providers
- Financial advisors

## Main Outcomes
- Save 4-8 hours of admin per new client
- Reduce missed onboarding steps and document chasing
- Improve client confidence immediately after signing
- Speed up first invoice/payment collection
- Give the owner clear visibility on onboarding status

## V1 Principles
- Keep Google Sheets as the operational hub.
- Use Google Drive folders and templates before adding complex client portals.
- Use OpenAI only for personalization and concise summaries.
- Keep payment handling practical with links from Yoco, PayFast, Ozow, Xero, QuickBooks, or the client's existing invoicing tool.
- Include POPIA-aware wording in client-facing messages.
