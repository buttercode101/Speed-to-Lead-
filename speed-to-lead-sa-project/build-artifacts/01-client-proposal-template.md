# Speed-to-Lead SA — Client Proposal Template

## 1. Executive Summary
{{ClientBusinessName}} receives inbound leads but currently loses revenue due to delayed response and inconsistent follow-up.

Speed-to-Lead SA will deploy a **Revenue Capture System** to:
- Capture every inbound lead in one place
- Alert staff instantly
- Acknowledge leads quickly
- Escalate missed responses
- Report weekly performance metrics

## 2. Scope of Work
### Included
- Intake setup (Form/WhatsApp source mapping)
- Lead log deployment (Google Sheets schema)
- Automation scenarios in Make.com
- Owner alerting setup (Gmail/WhatsApp)
- SLA rules and escalation workflow
- Weekly KPI summary report setup
- Team onboarding (1 session)

### Excluded (unless added)
- CRM migration
- Paid ads management
- Sales team hiring/training beyond handover
- Legal/compliance legal opinions

## 3. SLA Targets
- Lead capture to owner alert: < 60 seconds target
- First human response (business hours): < 5 minutes target
- Escalation trigger: not contacted by SLA threshold

## 4. Deliverables
1. Live lead capture endpoint(s)
2. Central lead log
3. Active automation scenarios (A-D)
4. Alert templates
5. Escalation SOP
6. KPI reporting dashboard/spec
7. Handover package + operator guide
